05

Why “Slowly Changing Dimension Type 2”?

Slowly changing describes dimension attributes—addresses, segments, marital status—that usually change less often than facts arrive. It does not mean the ETL is slow.

Type 1 overwrites the attribute. Type 2 expires the old row and inserts a new row with a new surrogate key, preserving what was true when each fact happened.
